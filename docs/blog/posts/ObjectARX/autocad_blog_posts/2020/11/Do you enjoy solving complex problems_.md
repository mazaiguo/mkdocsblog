Do you enjoy solving complex problems and troubleshooting is part of your gene?
You are a self-motivated, constant learner, courageous to explore new technologies.
If you feel you have these qualities, we have right job opening for you.
You as a member of the Developer Advocacy and Support (DAS) group, the Developer Consultant will deliver direct application programming evangelism to developers of third-party applications, based on Autodesk products, who are part of the Autodesk Developer Network (ADN) program and Forge platform.
The successful applicant will have a good grounding in C++, .NET, and object-oriented techniques, and show an aptitude for learning and problem-solving. The role will develop to include evangelizing the APIs to Autodesk’s products to prospective and existing ADN members through conference presentations, webcasts, training classes and one-on-one meetings.
Must Have:
VC++ and MFC
.NET and WPF
Working Knowledge of Visual Studio IDE
Working Knowledge of OOP and Design Patterns.
Good understanding of Data Structures and Algorithms.
Good to Have:
Experience in ObjectARX (AutoCAD SDK)
Experience in Web Technologies (HTML, CSS, and JS)
Experience in Git.
Job Location: Bengaluru, India.
