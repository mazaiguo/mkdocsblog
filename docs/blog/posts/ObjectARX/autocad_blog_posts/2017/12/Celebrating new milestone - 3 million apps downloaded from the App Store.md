# Celebrating new milestone - 3 million apps downloaded from the App Store

发布日期: 2017-12-01

## 文章内容

By Madhukar Moogala
We are proud and excited to announce that Autodesk App Store has exceeded Three million downloads. 
It is a huge milestone and we extend a big thank you to all our publishers for your contribution to the success of Autodesk App Store.
We encourage you to share the news with your customers too.
In addition to rapid growth in downloads, activity is growing across the board. 
There are now over 2950 apps in the store with 1200+ AutoCAD and its vertical apps, 900+ Revit Apps, 500+ Manufacturing apps and 250+ Media and Entertainment apps.
