# Fusion 360 Hackathon

发布日期: 2015-08-01

原始链接: https://adndevblog.typepad.com/autocad/2015/08/fusion-360-hackathon.html

## 文章内容

By Virupaksha Aithal
Want to get in on the ground floor on the future of design? 
Looking for a user base that embraces new ways of doing things? 
Have you taken a look at Fusion 360 yet – one of the first Cloud based mechanical design tools? 
This is a once in a decade opportunity to stake out your position in a new market – where you can be the market leader, an opportunity to leapfrog your competition. 
A small investment made early can lay a foundation for building a large new business long term . You can get a “quick start” building your first Fusion 360 app (using JavaScript, Python or C++) by participating in the upcoming Fusion 360 Hackathon. 
Build and publish a Fusion 360 app in the Autodesk App Store during the Hackathon and get yourself a $500 reward too! 
Looking for a simple app to get started with, get some ideas from the Fusion 360 IdeaStation and the Fusion 360 App Store.  Also be sure to check out Autodesk planned enhancements for Fusion 360 for the coming year (yes we publish our Fusion 360 development roadmap in public for all to see and comment).
Interested and want to learn more? Go to the Fusion 360 Hackathon website:
http://fusion360hackathon.com
You can find further information there about the event and can also register for it to attend the various webcasts and Q&A sessions that will be part of it.
See you at the event! :)
