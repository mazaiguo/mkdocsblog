# Autodesk Exchange Apps - Summary information

发布日期: 2012-07-01

原始链接: https://adndevblog.typepad.com/autocad/2012/07/autodesk-exchange-apps-summary-information.html

## 文章内容

By Stephen Preston
(I’m cross-posting this summary page to the AutoCAD, AEC and Manufacturing DevBlogs so the useful links are all in one place. We’ll be updating the Exchange Developer Center with the same information in due course, but that will take a few days. Once they’re posted, we’ll be keeping the Developer Center versions of this information up to date, so please use those in preference to the information posted here.)
Autodesk® Exchange Apps provides a centralized storefront for Autodesk customers to find apps that enhance their productivity using their Autodesk products. Apps can be anything a customer will find useful:
Plug-ins
Standalone applications
Training tutorials
E-books
Content
Etc.
If it’s something useful, then it’s a candidate for Autodesk Exchange Apps. Ask us if you’re not sure.
Anyone can submit a product to be published to Autodesk Exchange Apps. Simply go to apps.exchange.autodesk.com, select the Autodesk product your app is designed for, click on the ‘Exchange Apps Developers!’ link and follow the simple steps.
The Developer Center contains information to help you easily create or modify your apps to integrate with the simple Exchange Apps requirements and technical guidelines designed to give Exchange Apps customers a consistent user experience and to speed your product through the approval process. Please read the documentation provided here before you submit your app.
Product store specific information
Information for AutoCAD and AutoCAD vertical app developers
Information for Revit app developers
Information for Inventor app developers
Information for Vault app developers
General information for app publishers
App Submission Process Overview
App submissions – required information
Frequently Asked Questions
PayPal account setup
Icon guidelines
Training and Support
If you need more professional customization support, consider joining the Autodesk Developer Network.
Please email appsinfo@autodesk.com if you have any further questions after reviewing these guidelines and other documentation, or if you wish to submit an app for a product for which no specific guidelines are posted, or if the product you wish to post an app for isn’t listed on the Autodesk Exchange store.
Thank you for participating on Autodesk Exchange Apps.
