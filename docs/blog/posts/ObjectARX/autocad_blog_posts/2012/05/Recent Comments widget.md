## 文章内容

By Stephen Preston
Adam passed on a request to me from a private email conversation with Owen Wengerd:
"Please tell Stephen Preston that the blog should at least display recent comments on the main page so that readers can easily check for replies to their own comments."
Consider me told Owen :-). I've just added a widget over on the right sash. Its a bit ugly, but we can keep it if it helps. What does everyone think?
