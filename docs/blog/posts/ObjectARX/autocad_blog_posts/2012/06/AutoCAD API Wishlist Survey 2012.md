# AutoCAD API Wishlist Survey 2012

发布日期: 2012-06-01

原始链接: https://adndevblog.typepad.com/autocad/2012/06/autocad-api-wishlist-survey-2012.html

## 文章内容

By Stephen Preston
It is my pleasure to announce the launch of the AutoCAD API wishlist survey for 2012. We’re taking a slightly different approach for this year by asking some more general questions and focusing on future technologies, rather than specific AutoCAD features. I hope you’ll be willing to give 5-10 minutes of your time to take the survey – the results will be used to guide the efforts of our engineering team.
Here is the link to the survey - http://www.zoomerang.com/Survey/WEB22FYFDDAZ88.
The survey will remain open until June 22nd.
Update June 26th 2012: The survey is now closed. Thank you to everyone who participated.
