# Wishlist survey results are in

发布日期: 2012-06-01

原始链接: https://adndevblog.typepad.com/autocad/2012/06/wishlist-survey-results-are-in.html

## 文章内容

By Stephen Preston
Thank you to the 179 of you who took the time to complete this year's AutoCAD API Wishlist survey. We changed things around this year by asking some more forward looking questions, instead of the usual 'vanilla' feature hitlist. I'll be analyzing the survey data and passing the information on to our product management and development teams to help them make decisions about future features and APIs.
Click on the image of the survey results below to to open the full size version in a separate window. I'll refrain from adding my own commentary and leave you to draw our own conclusions.
