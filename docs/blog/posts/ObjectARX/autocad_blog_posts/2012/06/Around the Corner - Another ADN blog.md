# Around the Corner - Another ADN blog

发布日期: 2012-06-01

原始链接: https://adndevblog.typepad.com/autocad/2012/06/around-the-corner-another-adn-blog.html

## 文章内容

By Stephen Preston
It is my pleasure to announce another Autodesk developer blog - this time from Cyrille Fauvel and Kristine Middlemiss - our colleagues in the DevTech Media and Entertainment team.
Around the Corner is a blog focused on developing with Autodesk Maya, MotionBuilder, Mudbox and FBX.
Many of you will know Cyrille as a long-time ObjectARX guru. I'm not sure if Kristine has used AutoCAD much, but she knows a heck of a lot about the APIs she's writing about :-).
