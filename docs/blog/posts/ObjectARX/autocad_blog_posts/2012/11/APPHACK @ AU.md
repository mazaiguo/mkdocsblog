# APPHACK @ AU

发布日期: 2012-11-01

原始链接: https://adndevblog.typepad.com/autocad/2012/11/apphack-au.html

## 文章内容

By Stephen Preston
Slightly late to the party with this one (Kean blogged about this a few days ago), but anyway …
Here’s a chance to win US$5000 (and a lot of publicity) for your next great idea for an AutoCAD app.
APPHACK @ AU is a virtual code-fest to produce apps/plugins for AutoCAD using the ObjectARX and/or .NET APIs, and it's open to any ADN member who can produce a working app/plugin for AutoCAD. The event culminates in a live demo at Autodesk University in Las Vegas on November 26, 2012.  Simply submit your app and a demo video and you're in the running for some nice prizes. But hurry - the deadline for submissions is 11:59pm Pacific on Wednesday, 14 November, 2012.
First prize is US$5000, Second prize is US$2000, and Third prize is US$1000. Ten finalists will be awarded a newly released tablet computer valued at approximately $500.
Submit something you've already written or write something new. The theme for the event is Connecting AutoCAD to the Cloud.
In order to win, you must be present to perform a live demo of your app in Las Vegas on November 26. Click on the link below to visit the registration site for full details and the rules of engagement.
