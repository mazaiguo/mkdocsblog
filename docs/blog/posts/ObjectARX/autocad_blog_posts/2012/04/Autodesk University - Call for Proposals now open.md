# Autodesk University - Call for Proposals now open

发布日期: 2012-04-01

原始链接: https://adndevblog.typepad.com/autocad/2012/04/autodesk-university-call-for-proposals-now-open.html

## 文章内容

By Stephen Preston
The AU 2012 Call for Proposals is now open. Click on the link below to go to the AU website to find out more.
I've already submitted my usual "AutoCAD APIs: Meet the Experts" panel session proposal (described last year as 'the geekiest session at AU' :-D), and I've been walking around the San Rafael office today pestering some of the AutoCAD senior architects to sit on the panel. I'll also be proposing a few classes on publishing apps in the Exchange store.
Many of our DevTech team will be presenting classes at AU, as usual. We have a few classes on cloud and mobile development in the works this year (surprise, surprise). We'll be there anyway for the Autodesk Developer Network Annual Conference, and its fun to stay on to present and attend a few classes, and to meet lots of programmers using Autodesk APIs.
If you have some expertise (programming or otherwise) that you'd like to share, then why not submit a proposal? We're always looking for new and exciting classes.
