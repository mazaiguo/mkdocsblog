# AutoCAD Developer Documentation Survey

发布日期: 2016-03-01

原始链接: https://adndevblog.typepad.com/autocad/2016/03/autocad-developer-documentation-survey.html

## 文章内容

By Virupaksha Aithal
AutoCAD customization documentation team is conducting the survey on AutoCAD Developer Documentation. This survey is to collect information about your impression of the developer documentation and to determine our priorities. I hope you’ll be willing to give few minutes of your time to take the survey – the results will be used to guide the efforts of our developer documentation team.
Here is the link to the survey https://www.surveymonkey.com/r/QXNH7VQ. This survey will be active till 5/31/2016
Also, visit blog http://hyperpics.blogs.com/beyond_the_ui/2016/03/autocad-developer-documentation-survey-2016.html which lists the work done on previous developer documentation survey.
