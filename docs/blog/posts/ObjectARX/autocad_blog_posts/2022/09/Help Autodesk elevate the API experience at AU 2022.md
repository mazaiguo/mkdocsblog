## 文章内容

By Madhukar Moogala
When you are building automations, workflows, plugins, add-ons, and cloud-services, how do YOU design YOUR APIs? What guides and tools do you use? And what would you love to have to make your process better?
Who we're looking for:
Software developers working with Autodesk APIs and SDKs, in the AEC, PDM and M&E industries
My colleague Michelangelo Capraro, is hosting a workshop which is geared towards API users and developers - any of our customers developing tools using any of our SDKs or APIs - and trying to find out if there are things Autodesk can provide to make their work easier.
