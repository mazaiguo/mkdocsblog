# AU Call for Proposals closes soon

发布日期: 2013-05-01

原始链接: https://adndevblog.typepad.com/autocad/2013/05/au-call-for-proposals-closes-soon.html

## 文章内容

By Stephen Preston
A quick reminder that the AU Call for Proposals closes on May 19th, so you don't have much time if you want to be considered as a presenter this year.
We're keen to continue our theme from the last two years of scheduling a significant number of classes about general programming concepts and technologies in addition to classes about specific Autodesk APIs.
(Note that the 'Customization and Programming' track has been renamed to 'Developer' this year).
